Workout Plans

Train smart. Track better. Transform faster.

Chest Workout

Bench Press / Push-ups: 4×8–10
Incline Dumbbell Press: 3×10–12
Chest Fly (DB/Cable): 3×12–15
Machine Press / Dips: 3×10–12
Finisher: Push-ups to failure (2 rounds)

Leg Workout

Barbell Squats / Goblet Squats: 4×8–10
Leg Press: 3×12–15 (slow & deep)
Romanian Deadlifts: 3×10–12
Walking Lunges: 3×20 steps
Standing Calf Raises: 4×15–20

Shoulder Workout

Overhead Press (Barbell/Dumbbell):4×8–10
Lateral Raises: 4×12–15 (strict, no swing)
Front Raises (Plate/DB): 3×12
Rear Delt Fly / Face Pulls: 3×15
Upright Rows (EZ/DB): 3×10–12

Abs Workout

Crunches – slow & controlled
Leg Raises – keep lower back pressed down
Bicycle Crunch – twist, don’t rush
Plank – squeeze core + glutes
Mountain Climbers – fast but tight core

Triceps Workout

Bench/Chair Dips – elbows straight back
Close-Grip Push-ups – hands close, core tight
Diamond Push-ups (knees down if needed)
Triceps Kickbacks (water bottles if you want)

Back Workout

Lat Pulldown – pull to chest, squeeze lats
Seated Cable Row – chest up, elbows back
Dumbbell Bent-Over Row – slow & controlled
Face Pulls – rear delts + upper back

Bicep Workout

Barbell Curl
Concentration Curl (peak focus)
Preacher Curl Machine
eated Bicep Curl Machine

Forearm Workout

Wrist Curls (barbell or DB)
Reverse Wrist Curls
Hammer Curls
Reverse Barbell Curl
